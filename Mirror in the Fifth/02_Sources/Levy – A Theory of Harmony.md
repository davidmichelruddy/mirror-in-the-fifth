# Levy – A Theory of Harmony

## Citation

- Levy, Ernst. *A Theory of Harmony*. Edited by Siegmund Levarie. SUNY Series in Cultural Perspectives. Albany: State University of New York Press, 1985.
- Revision and translation of Levy's unpublished *Connaissance harmonique* (written 1940–41), built on Hans Kayser's harmonics (Foreword)
- Main file: 137 pp., calibre export, searchable. Print page numbers appear in the text as "Page N" markers; cite those, not the PDF page.
- Second copy: [[Theory of Harmony (scan).pdf]], 91 pp., scanned images without front matter. Printed page numbers are visible on the scan (e.g. scan p. 40 = book p. 46, the natural seventh)
