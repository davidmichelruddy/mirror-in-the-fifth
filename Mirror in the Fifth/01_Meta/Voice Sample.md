Folds across my pillow
State of Wakeful awareness
Contouring my face

Bright refracting beacon
Travelers unknown aloft
Flickers out of frame
 
Fresh from the oven
It looks and smells delicious
May we please have some?
  
Grabbing little hands
Seek my moles and blemishes 
Comfort to my Jo

I am a writer
I’ve decided to make it true
