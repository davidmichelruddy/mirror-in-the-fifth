# Tritone – character and resolution

- tritone required preparation – [[2026-10-31]]
- tritones resolve strongly to M3 or m6; weakly to m3 or M6 – requires one voice to move by whole step – [[2026-10-31]]
- octatonic scale = two diminished 7th chords – [[2026-10-31]]

> [!ai] Questions, flags, links
> - Checked: from ic6, both voices by half step lands on 4 (inward → M3) or 8 (outward → m6); m3 or M6 needs 3 or 9, so one voice must move 2. The arithmetic holds.
> - Checked: octatonic = union of two of the three dim7 collections, e.g. [0,3,6,9] ∪ [1,4,7,10]. Which two, and what the missing third does, is the question your trinity notes already ask.
> - Question: does "strong" here mean voice-leading economy (total motion 2 vs. 3), or something heard? The two may come apart.
> - Citation needed: the preparation rule. Look at Fux, *Gradus ad Parnassum* (1725), and how strict-counterpoint treatment of the tritone differs from the treatment of the dominant seventh.
