> [!ai] Questions, flags, links
> - Citation needed, devil: "diabolus in musica" seems to be a much later label than the medieval "mi contra fa" warnings; the moral-danger story is often overstated. Find the earliest attestation before using it.
> - Citation needed, cadence anecdote: usually told of the child Mozart getting out of bed to resolve a chord left hanging at the keyboard; tellings vary on who played it (Leopold? Nannerl?). Likely apocryphal; find the earliest printed version.
> - Citation needed, *Rite*: premiere 29 May 1913, Théâtre des Champs-Élysées. Which chord do you mean, the "Augurs" chord (F♭ major under E♭7)? Accounts also blame Nijinsky's choreography as much as the music, so check how much of the riot belongs to the harmony.
> - Each story is evidence about the ear, which is how it could serve the argument: the unresolved cadence as felt obligation (see [[Cadence and the balance of sides]]), the tritone as felt danger (see [[Tritone – character and resolution]]).
