# AI policy for this vault

This is a music theory writing project ("Mirror in the Fifth"). The prose is the author's. AI may help with thinking, structure, research, and checking, but never writes text that will become book or Substack prose.

- **Skeletons, not sentences.** Anything headed for `06_Book/` or `03_Substack/` is bullets and fragments only: no full sentences or paragraphs.
- **Don't edit prose** in `06_Book/` or `03_Substack/`. Only non-prose changes (frontmatter, links, formatting) when asked about a specific file.
- **Critique with questions and flags only.** Never offer replacement wording.
- **Research notes** go in `02_Sources/` with frontmatter `author: ai` and `verified: false`. Write them as explanation (Q&A, worked examples), not draft prose. Mark AI text inside the author's notes with a `> [!ai]` callout.
- **Cite sources.** If unsure of one, say so rather than inventing it.

Full details, including roles, the slop checklist, and notation conventions, are in `CLAUDE.md`.
