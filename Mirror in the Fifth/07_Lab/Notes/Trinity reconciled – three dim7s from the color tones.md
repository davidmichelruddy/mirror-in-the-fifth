## 2. The rebuild: each dim7 held by two color tones

- Three anchors → three pairs → three dim7s; each anchor gives its bright tone to one engine, its dark tone to another
- Each engine: one bright holder, one dark holder
- Each engine resolves (inner holder) to the lower pole of the anchor it excludes
  - C°7: E♭ → G (D's pole) · C♯°7: E → F (S's pole) · B°7: B, A♭ → C (T's pole)
  - outer holders (A, B♭) land one fifth beyond the window: B♭, D
- → "two anchors hold, the third receives"
- Engine 2 of the Trinity (S & D → B°7 → home): survives intact, now derived

**Why three, and why these** (mod 3 = which dim7)

- Lower pole *p*: dark tone *p*+3 ≡ *p*, bright tone *p*+4 ≡ *p*+1 (mod 3)
- Fifth = 7 ≡ 1 (mod 3) → three consecutive anchors F, C, G hit residues 2, 0, 1 → bright tones fill all three dim7s, dark tones fill all three, offset by one
- Bright tone of *p* and dark tone of *p*+7 = a tritone apart (*p*+4, *p*+10) → same held tritone
  - F's bright A + C's dark E♭ → C°7
  - C's bright E + G's dark B♭ → C♯°7
- The window's loose ends (G's bright B, F's dark A♭) have no such partner inside → their tritone partners are the outer poles F, D → B°7
- → C°7, C♯°7: links inside the window · B°7: the window's two ends folded together
- General: true for any three-anchor window; with home in the middle, B°7 = the set home never holds

## 4. Home's mirror and the lattice

- → B°7 = the only dim7 fixed by home's mirror; C°7 ↔ C♯°7 a mirror pair

- Mirror pair = family 1 (E, E♭, B♭, A, spirals note §6) split by dim7; self-mirrored = family 2 (B, A♭)
